Why are you here?
Not to get rich. Not to be famous.
Allah answered this question Himself. In one verse.
Allah, the Exalted, says in Surat adh-Dhariyat. The meaning of the verse in English:
"I have only created Jinns and men, that they may serve Me."
Did you hear the word "only"?
Not "also". Not "sometimes". Only.
The commentator of Fath al-Majid explains that Allah created jinn and humans just to worship Him. Then he says: "That is the wisdom behind their creation."
Worship Him. And worship Him alone.
That word, alone, has a name: Tawhid.
And it is the reason you are here.
